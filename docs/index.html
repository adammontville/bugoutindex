About this index

The BugOut Index is a directional stress and stability reading (methodology v1.0.0). Six published statistics sit between fixed endpoints and combine into one 0–100 score. Higher means those conditions are less stressed. The number shows how hard the published inputs are pressing. It is not a forecast, and it is not an instruction to bug out. Markets, the short-term pulse, labor utilization, and food prices on this page are companions. They are not in the score. Several inputs update monthly, quarterly, or once a year, so many weeks the number does not move. See methodology for the calculation, weights, and limits. The committed weekly files are the record of a past score. Rerunning the publisher fetches current observations and does not rebuild that week.

For the long-horizon structural picture — whether the country is currently set up to produce broadly-shared prosperity and mobility over years and decades — see the American Dream Reality Index (ADRI). ADRI updates annually and weights toward education, health, real income, employment, inequality, and civic and safety fundamentals. The two indexes answer different questions. The BugOut Index is a weekly reading of pressure in a few published series. ADRI moves on slow structural conditions. Each can move for reasons the other does not catch.
